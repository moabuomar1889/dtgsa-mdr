# Separation of Duties

Default conflicts prevent the same person serving as Prepared By Manager and
Reviewer, Reviewer and Approver, Approver and DC Validator, or Prepared By
Manager and DC Validator. Evaluation occurs at preparation, submission,
reassignment, and decision.

An emergency exception is case-specific, reasoned, expiring, visible in the
audit/event stream, and requires approval by a different administrator. The
requester cannot self-approve. Reassignment and delegation retain reason,
changer, effective dates, original assignment history, and notification events.
